Twelve years of building a Guatemalan company

Corporación Caraliant, S.A. was founded in 2014 with a simple idea: serve traditional retail reliably and on time. Today, we bring manufacturing, sales and distribution together in one business model.

Our mission

To produce and distribute high-quality consumer goods with responsive, personalized service that helps our customers grow throughout Guatemala.

Our vision

To be Guatemala’s leading consumer goods manufacturing and distribution corporation, with our own brands recognized at home and abroad.

Quality
Strict standards for every batch we produce and every brand we represent.
Trust
Long-term relationships with customers, suppliers and team members.
Agility
On-time deliveries and a prompt response at every retail outlet.
Customer focus
Personalized service for store owners, wholesalers and distribution partners.
Our story

From distribution to manufacturing and new markets

Since 2014, CORCA has strengthened its distribution network, developed its industrial capacity and built strategic partnerships. With logistics that combine motorcycles and delivery vans, we continue to expand our coverage in Guatemala and our presence in regional and international markets.

2014
CORCA is founded
Founded on February 10, 2014 to distribute snacks, chocolates, candy and edible oils, with an innovative network of 40 motorcycles serving traditional retail.
2018
Portfolio expansion
We expanded the brands we represent and added distribution centers to reach more departments across the country.
2022
We enter manufacturing
We acquired the Chao Mein noodle plant and launched our own brands, developed for Guatemalan tastes.
2023
Contract manufacturing agreements
We began manufacturing for leading companies in Guatemala and Central America, building on our industrial capacity.
2025
Delivery van fleet
Broader geographic coverage, higher-volume customers and faster deliveries.
2026
Exports and partnerships
We began exporting Chao Mein to the United States and established a partnership with Grupo BOCEL for pasta in Guatemala, El Salvador and Honduras.
